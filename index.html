Two quick rules keep your account low-key while our AI does the outfit work.

Skip the face photos

Every closet photo is read by an AI vision model (Gemini) to tag what you're wearing. Crop out faces, or shoot flatlays and hangers instead.

Go by a nickname

Use a display name, not your full real name — it's what shows on your profile and feed posts.

Why: uploaded photos leave the app to get analyzed by a third-party AI model. Keeping faces and real names out just limits what's floating around outside FitVault.
